# Community code of conduct

Be respectful, specific, and constructive.

We welcome questions, criticism, and disagreement about product behavior and direction. Harassment, discrimination, threats, spam, exposure of private information, and intentionally disruptive behavior are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove content that violates these expectations and may restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

Report conduct concerns privately through [All2CF Support](https://app.all2cf.com/?support=ticket&source=github-conduct).
