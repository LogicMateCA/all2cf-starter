# Licensing guide

All2CF Starter is offered under a dual-license model. This guide explains intended use; the license texts control if this guide conflicts with them.

## Free AGPL path

Use the repository under AGPL-3.0-or-later when you are willing to comply with that license. Commercial activity is allowed under AGPL. Network users may be entitled to receive the corresponding source for a modified network service, and distribution can create additional source and notice obligations.

Choose AGPL when the product and its Starter-derived modifications can remain AGPL-compatible.

## $199 commercial path

Purchase one commercial license for each independent product that needs to remain proprietary or otherwise operate without AGPL obligations for Starter-owned code.

One licensed product includes:

- one named end-user product or SaaS offering;
- its local, CI, Development, Preview, Staging, and Production environments;
- Web, API, iOS, Android, and mobile-Web surfaces belonging to that same product;
- unlimited employees and contractors working for the licensee on that product;
- modification and private distribution of Starter-derived code as part of that product.

Separate products, client products, white-label products sold as distinct offerings, and a template/generator offered to third parties each require a separate commercial license.

The commercial license does not permit reselling or sublicensing All2CF Starter, Setup, the Engine, Packs, or substantially equivalent boilerplate as a competing starter kit, code generator, marketplace template, or hosted generation service.

## Generated project ownership

You own original business logic, content, branding, data, and other original material added to a generated project. Starter-owned code remains licensed, not sold. A commercial license permits Starter-owned portions of the licensed product to remain closed source under its terms.

Selecting only AGPL does not automatically make unrelated original code subject to AGPL in every circumstance; actual obligations depend on how code is combined, modified, conveyed, and offered over a network. Obtain legal advice for your distribution model.

## Updates and services

A commercial license is perpetual for the Starter version and materials delivered under the purchase. Future major versions, premium Packs, managed updates, All2CF hosting, Visual services, deployment work, and support are not included unless the order or invoice says otherwise.

Generated projects remain independently runnable. A commercial license does not require an All2CF connection.

## Third-party software

The commercial license covers only code for which the licensor has commercial relicensing authority. Dependencies, copied assets, fonts, donor material, and other third-party components retain their own licenses and notices. Their terms are not replaced by either All2CF license path.

## Examples

- One company SaaS with Web and iOS/Android apps: one commercial license.
- The same product deployed to Development and Production: still one license.
- An agency building three unrelated client products: three licenses.
- A company operating two separately marketed SaaS products: two licenses.
- Selling a customized copy of Starter as another starter kit: not allowed by the standard commercial license.

## Purchasing

The purchaser, licensed product, license date, covered Starter version, and payment are identified by the commercial order or invoice. The list price is US$199 per independent product. Add the final purchase URL and support contact before public launch.
